## Two operational facts worth more than their size

this guest". That is FALSE for this configuration.** A `send-key ret`
dismissed a modal alert that had wedged a machine — with `askguest` and
the anchor worker both starved behind it and `shutdown-guest.py` unable
to run. **That is the escape hatch when a modal wedges a VM**, and the
header currently tells the next person it does not exist.

**A constant can be documented as suspect and still never checked.**
`Platinum`'s highlight was `0xCCCCFF`, extracted from the theme *file*;
the machine answers **`#97A1DE`** — off by R−53 G−43 B−33 — and the guest's
own pixels agree with the machine over 2399 of 3240 pixels of a selected
row. The code's own comment had already named the seam (*"this is what
the theme file SAYS, not what a running AppearanceLib answers"*), and the
test had already recorded that **no capture in the corpus carried a
selected list row**, so forcing the constant to magenta changed no pixel
anywhere. **It had never been seen on a screen.** The other three colours
we were carrying turned out right; this one was wrong for two years of
nobody being able to see it.

The line that lane drew for what belongs on the wire is worth keeping:
**does a Toolbox call answer it?** Fills the machine makes and hands out
go on the wire; the bevel ramp, the menu-title fill and every inset stay
constants, because they are our drawing of an idiom rather than a fill
any guest made.

## Sweep B's regressions: four symptoms, two mechanisms

**Mechanism 1 — rung 2 drew over rung 1 because nothing asked.**
`SceneRenderer.drawControl` had **never consulted `DisplayReplay.Coverage`
at all**; its twin `drawDialogItem` has since 2026-08-06. **Two readers of
one policy, one of them not implementing it.** And the test the other
reader does ask, `Coverage.mostlyCovers`, asks the **rectangle's**
question rather than the **text's** — a DITL row is a slack box, so
Memory's `"Disk Cache"` row is 102pt holding a 48pt run (47%, so it said
no), and a multi-line row never reaches half at all.

**Mechanism 2 — `derived` is a knowledge level and was read as two other
things.** As *words*: the CDEF branch emitted `GetControlValue` into
`semantic.value`, which the renderer draws as display text, so every
static label arrived carrying `"0"`. As *not good enough*:
`semanticControlRefs` required `knowledge == .known`, so every
CDEF-classified control lost its rectangle to a dialog item carrying
`kind: null`.

R1 and 3a are mechanism 1; R2 and 3b are mechanism 2. **Memory's doubling
and stroke are gone, `^1K` resolves to the machine's `8160K`, NOW's own
Workshop was silently doubled too and is now clean, and Monitors gained
its real group boxes.** Date & Time, SimpleText and Finder: **0 pixels
changed** — no collateral.

**A visible trade Michelle should see rather than be told about:** OS 9
builds Memory's radios from a CDEF the guest reports as the button
*family*, so six arrive as `pushButton`. With the Platinum pill correctly
withheld, **the machine's radio mark does not reach the replay either** —
so those rows are now a bare label where the machine draws a filled
circle. Before, they were a pill: *a confident wrong answer, not a better
one.* Two separable items behind it (a classification the guest cannot
make honestly, and a capture gap), both now visible instead of hidden.

**Still live, and it is the same defect in the twin:**
`drawDialogItem`'s `pushButton` branch **still has no coverage gate at
all**, where its counterpart now does. Also open: `Coverage.mostlyCovers`
remains a single-rect test everywhere it is not about text, and the
radio-CDEF classification.
